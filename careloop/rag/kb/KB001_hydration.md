Drinking enough water each day is important for your overall health. Most adults should aim for 6 to 8 glasses of water daily. Water helps your body regulate temperature, carry nutrients, and remove waste. Older adults may not feel as thirsty even when their bodies need water, so it is helpful to drink water regularly throughout the day, even without feeling thirsty. Herbal teas and soups can also count toward fluid intake. Signs of low hydration include dark urine, feeling tired, or dry mouth.
