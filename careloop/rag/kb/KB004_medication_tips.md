Taking your medications as prescribed is very important for managing your health condition. Try to take them at the same time every day to build a routine. A pill organizer or alarm on your phone can help you remember. Never stop taking or change a medication without speaking to your doctor or pharmacist first. If you miss a dose, take it as soon as you remember — but if it is nearly time for your next dose, skip the missed one and continue as normal. Do not double up. If you experience any new or unusual symptoms after starting a medication, let your healthcare team know as soon as possible.
