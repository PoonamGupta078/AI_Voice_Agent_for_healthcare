Good sleep is important for health at every age. Adults generally need 7 to 9 hours of sleep per night, though some older adults find they sleep a little less than this. To improve sleep, try going to bed and waking up at the same time every day, even on weekends. Keep your bedroom dark, quiet, and cool. Avoid caffeine in the afternoon or evening. Gentle stretching or a short walk during the day can help you sleep better at night. If you are having trouble sleeping regularly, please let your doctor know, as there may be an underlying reason that can be helped.
