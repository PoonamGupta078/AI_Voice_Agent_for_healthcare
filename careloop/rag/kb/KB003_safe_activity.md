Staying active is one of the best things you can do for your health as you get older. Even gentle movement, like a short daily walk, can improve your strength, balance, and mood. Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity per week — that is about 20 to 30 minutes most days. If walking feels hard, start with 5 to 10 minutes and slowly build up. Chair-based exercises and stretching are also helpful. Always start slowly and stop if you feel chest pain, dizziness, or severe shortness of breath. Talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise routine.
