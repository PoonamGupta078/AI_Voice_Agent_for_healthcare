Getting the most from your doctor's visit takes a little preparation. Before you go, write down any new symptoms you have noticed, even if you are not sure they are important. Note when the symptom started, how often it happens, and what makes it better or worse. Bring a list of all your medications, including vitamins and supplements. Write down any questions you want to ask — it is easy to forget in the moment. You can also ask a friend or family member to come with you for support. During the visit, do not be afraid to ask your doctor to explain anything you do not understand.
